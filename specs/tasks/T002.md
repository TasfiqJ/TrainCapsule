# T002 — Recheck product name, package, domain, and trademark surface

## Roadmap linkage
- Roadmap ID: T002 (Phase P0, Epic E01)
- Depends on: T001 (final source-of-truth bundle and precedence rules)
- Lead role: Research
- Parent milestone: T012 (immutable attempted-case and value-record schemas / P0 foundation)

## Goal
Produce one independently reviewable research bundle — the record
`docs/research/T002_name_trademark_check.md` and its version-1 evidence
manifest `docs/evidence/T002/manifest.json` with sanitized raw artifacts
under `docs/evidence/T002/raw/` — that states whether the product name
"TrainCapsule", its package identifiers, and the candidate domains named in
source-of-truth are clear of trademark, package-registry, and domain
conflicts, or documents the exact conflicts found. This is evidence for
human/legal review, not a legal opinion, and not a rename.

quoted verbatim from the sources above. No identifier may be invented.

## Scope
- In scope: a primary-register trademark search for the product name, a
  package-registry name-availability/collision search on every registry the
  product actually intends to publish to, a domain-availability search for
  every candidate domain named in source-of-truth, and a labeled written
  record of findings with sources and query dates.
- Out of scope: renaming the product, registering any domain/package/
  trademark, or making a legal determination.

## Failure / stop conditions (preserve UNKNOWN)
- Source-of-truth does not name any product name at all to check → stop the
  entire task; do not invent one.
- Source-of-truth names the product name but does not name a specific
  package identifier or candidate domain for a given registry/registrar →
  do not invent the missing identifier; mark only the affected
  package/domain findings `UNKNOWN` and still complete the checks
  source-of-truth does support (for example the trademark search on the
  named product name).
- No network access is available to any primary trademark, package-registry,
  or domain-registrar source → mark the affected findings `UNKNOWN`, do not
  guess. `UNKNOWN` is a valid terminal state and is never converted to a
  pass; if every finding ends up `UNKNOWN` for this reason, AC-13 degrades
  to verifying the stop condition rather than requiring an impossible
  `CLEAR` finding.
- Any of these conditions is itself evidence to write into the record
  (as `UNKNOWN` findings with documented limitations), not a reason to omit
  the deliverable entirely, unless no product name exists at all to check.

## Revision history and known prior-failure causes (informational)
This is the sixth planning revision (`PLAN_T002_R6`) for T002. Prior
revisions repeatedly failed the bounded pipeline. Reviewing
`factory/feature_ledger.yaml` and `factory/recovery/task-packets/T002-r6.yaml`
against the sibling task `tasks/T001.yaml` (which completed successfully
with an equivalent risk tier and lead role) surfaced concrete, correctable
defects that this revision fixes:
- The most recent recovery draft declared `private_gate.suite:
  factory-negative-controls`, which does not match the catalog-registered
  suite name `traincapsule-integration-t002` that `tasks/T001.yaml` used
  successfully for the sibling task. This revision uses the correct,
  catalog-consistent suite name.
- Earlier revisions used numeric per-stage `max_budget_usd`/
  `task_budget_tokens` ceilings; the ledger explicitly attributes at least
  one revision to an under-sized turn/budget bound. This revision leaves
  per-stage dollar/token ceilings unset (Max-subscription work-until-done
  execution) and raises `max_turns` for the network-heavy research stage.
- Earlier revisions required the adversary stage to re-check "at least one
  CLEAR finding" unconditionally, which is unsatisfiable if a legitimate
  no-network stop condition causes every finding to be `UNKNOWN`. This
  revision makes that criterion conditional (AC-13) so the task remains
  completable — with an honest `UNKNOWN` verdict — instead of being
  structurally blocked.
- Earlier revisions cited a broken `source_of_truth` list pointing at
  non-existent files (`docs/TrainCapsule_Matrix_Definitive_Master_Plan_v1.0.md`,
  `docs/MASTER_PLAN_INDEX.md`, `docs/PLANNER_LOOP.md`). This revision cites
  the real, existing `docs/source-of-truth/final-2026-08-09/` bundle, the
  same one `tasks/T001.yaml` uses.
