# 12 — Roadmap, Backlog, and Master Build Prompt

- **Document date:** 9 August 2026
- **Status:** **Build authorized**
- **Execution posture:** build the complete local product first; search for commercial proof in parallel and intensify outreach after a credible alpha
- **Autonomous rule:** absence of customer evidence blocks claims and customer-specific expansion, not independent local engineering

## 12.1 Governing roadmap rule

> **Build the reusable Close–Qualify–Exchange kernel, continuously benchmark it against native and emerging systems, and allow evidence to replace the wedge without discarding the stable trust core.**

The roadmap is deliberately different from “validate everything before coding.” The AI factory may execute Phases P0–P9 without a design partner. It must not invent external results, payment, provider acceptance, or production value.

## 12.5 Technical gates

### G1 — identity and evidence

- deterministic workload/change locks;
- exact backend/pack/policy identity;
- no silent loss;
- customer can inspect capture;
- private data remains local.

### G2 — observed boundary

- supported hidden faults located correctly;
- no clean control blamed;
- first observed boundary not labeled root cause;
- alternatives and gaps preserved.

### G3 — faithful experiment

- material reduction in at least one cost dimension;
- declared predicate and applicability preserved;
- illegal reductions rejected;
- environment and private-reference state explicit.

### G4 — recovery and qualification

- named state properties;
- canary and rollback;
- false-green faults rejected;
- stale/out-of-envelope contracts blocked;
- qualification result bounded.

### G5 — security

- sandbox and archive tests;
- provenance/signing;
- no private export contamination;
- no model-generated executable;
- zero unresolved critical/high trust-path vulnerabilities.

### G6 — operator independence

- clean installation;
- independent execution;
- no hidden founder edits;
- same statuses across all surfaces.

### G7 — complete-substitute advantage

TrainCapsule must beat or uniquely complete the combination of:

```text
current framework tools
+ accelerator/device tools
+ cloud/platform features
+ contracted support
+ internal experts
+ approved coding/operations agents
+ relevant research backends
```

Beating raw logs is not sufficient.

### G8 — commercial repetition

- real material result;
- second paid/sponsored incident or qualification;
- or recurring protected-workload/provider commitment;
- and acceptable delivery economics.

## 12.11 Autonomous wedge-review protocol

Every month, or after any major native/research release, the product and adversarial agents must update:

- exact new capability;
- whether it duplicates a TrainCapsule subsystem;
- migration/integration cost;
- remaining customer outcome;
- effect on price and defensibility;
- current incident-corpus evidence;
- current external signal.

Required disposition:

```text
KEEP
INTEGRATE_EXISTING_BACKEND
UPSTREAM
NARROW
REPLACE_WEDGE
PAUSE
STOP
```

The release agent blocks further work on a deprecated wedge except migration, documentation, and safe removal.

## 12.13 Final execution directive

> Build the complete local system. Keep the incident packs narrow. Treat every technical primitive as replaceable. Make failure-derived qualification—not emergency diagnosis alone—the recurring product. Let real customer behavior decide whether the company becomes software, a productized service, an open-source career asset, a redesigned wedge, or a stopped effort.
