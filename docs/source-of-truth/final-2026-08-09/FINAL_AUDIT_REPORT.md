## 1. Scope audit

The final set consistently defines TrainCapsule as:

> **Accelerated Workload Failure Reproduction and Change Qualification**

The three product loops are:

- **Close:** unresolved failure to bounded experiment, Recovery Assurance, and incident contract;
- **Qualify:** incident/critical-workload contract against a software, hardware, topology, checkpoint, cloud, or workload change;
- **Exchange:** policy-approved native or federated evidence for external organizations.

The initial incident packs are consistently:

1. `PRE_COLLECTIVE_LIFECYCLE_CONTRACT_V1`
2. `CHECKPOINT_RESUME_STATE_CONSISTENCY_V1`

No first-release commitment remains to broad numerical, fail-slow, or hardware packs. Those are evidence-selected and backend-assisted.

## 2. Fatal-flaw corrections verified

The documents now address:

- rare-incident-only recurring revenue;
- improving native recovery;
- bundled cloud/platform economics;
- cross-company acceptance deadlock;
- first observed divergence versus root cause;
- unsafe or impossible portable reproduction;
- instrumentation perturbation;
- false assurance from reduced tests;
- artifact decay;
- adapter churn;
- procurement/incident urgency;
- upstream absorption into free tests;
- customer preference for mitigation;
- managed platforms removing deployment authority;
- AI agents commoditizing narrative diagnosis;
- research systems occupying operator alignment and scale emulation;
- founder dependence and AI-authored-code credibility.

## 3. Defensibility audit

The plan no longer treats the following as durable moats:

- recorder;
- logs;
- event graph;
- LLM diagnosis;
- one divergence algorithm;
- one reducer;
- one replay engine;
- one portable file;
- one upstream test.

The defensible boundary is consistently:

```text
identity and provenance
+ controlled experiments
+ faithfulness and applicability
+ Recovery Assurance
+ incident-contract lifecycle
+ change qualification
+ customer-local/federated execution
+ private integrations
+ operational trust
```

OpGuard and PrismLLM are treated as benchmark/integration backends, not ignored overlap or proprietary TrainCapsule inventions.

## 4. Build-first audit

The final plan authorizes the complete local P0–P9 build without customer proof.

It blocks:

- fabricated external facts;
- expensive provider/hosted breadth;
- broad framework/accelerator support;
- owned GPU fleet;
- autonomous production repair;
- commercial and acquisition claims.

The AI loop must continue independent work while marking customer-only evidence `EXTERNAL_VALIDATION_REQUIRED`.

## 5. Truth and safety audit

Verified requirements include:

- raw evidence preserved;
- missing evidence remains missing;
- infrastructure errors cannot become passes;
- first observed divergence is not root cause;
- no global-minimum reproducer language;
- `UNKNOWN` is terminal and visible;
- Recovery Assurance names tested properties;
- applicability envelopes constrain conclusions;
- stale/expired/disputed/revoked contracts cannot qualify changes;
- no hardware confirmation from telemetry alone;
- all failed, rejected, no-gain, and unknown cases remain in the denominator;
- local/federated data handling is default;
- portable artifacts require policy, integrity, security, and verification.

## 6. Commercial audit

The business sequence is consistent:

```text
assessment
→ bounded closure
→ failure-derived contract
→ paid change qualification
→ critical-workload assurance
→ provider evidence gateway
```

Annual recurring revenue is not assumed before demonstrated closure or qualification value.

The plan permits a productized-service path when scope and margin are strong, but forbids calling founder-dependent bespoke investigation SaaS.

## 7. Acquisition audit

All numeric acquisition-probability estimates were removed.

The acquisition thesis now requires:

- customer value first;
- repeat operational use;
- deployed contracts;
- private integrations;
- open authority where useful;
- measured value;
- team beyond founder;
- partnership and buyer-specific ownership advantage.

Brev is used only as a workflow/platform-attach pattern, not as proof or a product template.

## 8. Career audit

The career thesis distinguishes:

- generated repository;
- real local multi-GPU system;
- cross-node or scale-faithful case;
- upstream acceptance;
- external use;
- recurring product.

It adds a founder technical-defense requirement so autonomous coding does not create a project the founder cannot explain.

## Final audit decision

**The final plan is sufficiently coherent and option-preserving to begin the autonomous build.**

It is not a guarantee of commercial success. It is a materially stronger build bet because the same core can:

- close an incident;
- qualify future change;
- integrate emerging technical backends;
- rotate to a stronger incident pack;
- produce open-source/career value;
- and stop cleanly when the economics fail.
