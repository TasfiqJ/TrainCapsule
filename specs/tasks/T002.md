# T002 — Recheck product name, package, domain, and trademark surface

## Roadmap linkage
- Roadmap ID: T002 (Phase P0)
- Depends on: T001
- Lead role: Research

## Outcome
Produce one independently reviewable research record,
`docs/research/T002_name_trademark_check.md`, that states whether the
product name "TrainCapsule", its package identifiers, and candidate
domains (as named in source-of-truth) are clear of trademark,
package-registry, and domain conflicts — or documents the exact
conflicts found. This is evidence for human/legal decision-making, not
a legal opinion and not a rename.

## Scope
- In scope: trademark register search, package-registry
  name-availability search, domain-availability search, and a labeled
  written record of findings with sources and dates.
- Out of scope: renaming the product, registering any domain/package/
  trademark, or making a legal determination.

## Machine-verifiable acceptance criteria
1. `docs/research/T002_name_trademark_check.md` exists.
2. The record quotes the exact product name and package identifiers
   under review from source-of-truth.
3. Trademark search results are documented for at least one primary
   trademark register, with source URLs and query dates.
4. Package-name availability is documented for every registry the
   product actually intends to publish to, with source URLs and dates.
5. Domain availability is documented for each candidate domain named
   in source-of-truth, with source URLs and dates.
6. Every finding is labeled `CLEAR`, `CONFLICT`, or `UNKNOWN`; `UNKNOWN`
   is used whenever a check could not be completed and is never
   silently upgraded to `CLEAR`.
7. Any `CONFLICT` finding names the conflicting party/registrant, an
   evidence link, and a plain description of the collision.
8. The record includes a line `Overall verdict: clear|conflicts_found|unknown`
   computed only from the itemized findings.
9. Every source cited is within the allowed research domains.
10. No file outside `docs/research/T002_name_trademark_check.md` is
    created or modified by the research stage.
11. The adversary stage independently re-checks at least one `CLEAR`
    finding and confirms or disputes it with its own evidence link.
12. The audit stage verifies every finding cites a working evidence
    link and that no `CONFLICT`/`UNKNOWN` was silently converted to
    `CLEAR`.
13. The release stage only reads the record and machine gate output.

## Stop conditions (preserve UNKNOWN)
- Source-of-truth does not name a definitive product name, package
  identifier, or candidate domain list to check → stop, do not invent
  the missing names.
- No network access is available to any primary trademark,
  package-registry, or domain-registrar source → mark the affected
  findings `UNKNOWN`, do not guess.

## Allowed paths
- Research stage may write only: `docs/research/T002_name_trademark_check.md`
- Adversary, audit, and release stages are read-only (no write paths).

## Forbidden paths
`.claude/**`, `config/**`, `prompts/**`, `tcfactory/**`, `schemas/**`,
`factory/state/**`, `factory/queue/**`, `factory/proposals/**`,
`factory/feature_ledger.yaml`, `factory/product_definition_of_done.yaml`,
`docs/source-of-truth/**`, `specs/**` (except this file, owned by the
planner, not by T002 stages).

## Deterministic gates
- `test -f docs/research/T002_name_trademark_check.md`
- `grep -Eq 'Overall verdict:\s*(clear|conflicts_found|unknown)' docs/research/T002_name_trademark_check.md`
- `! grep -Eiq 'UNKNOWN[^\n]*(upgraded|converted|treated as)[^\n]*CLEAR' docs/research/T002_name_trademark_check.md`

## Value contract
- Mode: foundational (required dependency; not independently monetized).
- Evidence path: `docs/research/T002_name_trademark_check.md`.
- Falsification: any `CONFLICT`/`UNKNOWN` finding present while the
  overall verdict reads `clear` invalidates the record.
