# TrainCapsule product fixtures

These fixtures exercise the bounded V3 product spine without a network, SaaS account,
training runner, baseline/candidate runner, or reduction engine.

- `flight-recorder/supported/` is the controlled TrainCapsule `1.0` adapter fixture.
- `flight-recorder/real-format/` exercises the public PyTorch Flight Recorder JSON field shape
  (`version`, `pytorch_version`, `pg_config`, `collective_seq_id`, tensor fields, and frames).
- `flight-recorder/unsupported/` proves that an unknown version stops as
  `UNSUPPORTED_VERSION`; it is never guessed or silently upgraded.
- The product journey tests construct eligible, missing-evidence, native-sufficient,
  policy-blocked, unknown, expired, and malicious archive/path cases from these fixtures.

All timestamps and identities used by the fixtures are explicit. Customer evidence remains
under the caller-selected local evidence-store path.

Identity fixtures provide raw decision-relevant variables. The product computes versioned redacted
digests and derives workload and environment identity strength from evidence policy; it does not
trust caller-supplied binding booleans or strength labels.
The real-format fixture is adapter input, not proof of a root cause or a complete capture. The
generated native baseline attributes only direct native-tool observations, carries limitations and
unresolved questions, and records both elapsed time and operator effort. Preflight then requires
case-, workload-, and baseline/candidate-environment-bound CAS objects, including content-addressed
materialization recipes for both environments. It re-imports the raw evidence and recomputes the
fixed pack-versioned completeness roster, source/tool versions, missing ranks, findings, unknowns,
and native sufficiency. Deterministic policy-verifier records and native sufficiency provenance are
generated by the engine, never accepted as caller verdicts; no human-availability field or human
approval path exists in the executable decision engine.

The adapter consumes directories only. ZIP/tar archives are truthfully unsupported and are never
extracted; symlinked or escaping trace content is rejected.
